# RESEARCH_PLAN

## §1 Overall Objective

Populate Atlas across every topic named in RESEARCH_QUEUE, in the sequence RESEARCH_QUEUE prescribes, following the workflow defined in RESEARCH_EXECUTION and the standards defined in RESEARCH_METHOD. The plan's completion state is Atlas Version 1: every RESEARCH_QUEUE topic carries at least one admitted entry, every dependency has been honored, and every surfacing threshold met has been recorded.

The plan does not commit to a calendar. It commits to a sequence and to the review gates that separate its phases.

## §2 Estimated Phases

The plan is divided into four phases that correspond to the three levels of RESEARCH_QUEUE plus a closing review.

- **Phase A — Foundation.** Level 1 topics investigated to first admission.
- **Phase B — Transformation.** Level 2 topics investigated to first admission.
- **Phase C — Product Understanding.** Level 3 topics investigated to first admission.
- **Phase D — Atlas v1 Ratification.** Cross-cutting review that confirms every topic has been admitted, every dependency honored, and every surfacing recorded.

Phase boundaries are enforced by review gates (§6). A phase does not close until its gate is passed.

## §3 Research Sequence

Sequence follows RESEARCH_QUEUE. Within each phase, topics are ordered by their dependency graph as defined in RESEARCH_QUEUE: a topic begins only after every topic it depends on has been admitted to Atlas. One topic is executed at a time. No parallel research.

### Phase A — Foundation (Level 1)

Order of execution:

1. **Human Nature (1.1).** First because every downstream topic depends on it.
2. **Attention (1.5).** Second because Decision Making depends on it and it has no other Level-1 dependencies beyond Human Nature.
3. **Emotion (1.6).** Third because multiple Level-2 topics depend on it and it depends only on Human Nature.
4. **Motivation (1.2).** Fourth because Level-2 depends on it and it depends only on Human Nature.
5. **Decision Making (1.4).** Fifth because it depends on Human Nature and Attention.
6. **Identity (1.3).** Sixth because it depends on Human Nature and Motivation.

Rationale for the order: after Human Nature grounds the phase, topics with the fewest intra-level dependencies (Attention, Emotion) are investigated before topics that depend on them (Decision Making, Identity).

### Phase B — Transformation (Level 2)

Order of execution:

1. **Self-Trust (2.1).** First because it is the primary metric that grounds every other Level-2 topic and every Level-3 topic.
2. **Behavior Change (2.2).** Second because Habits, Addiction, Performance, and multiple Level-3 topics depend on it.
3. **Habits (2.3).** Third because Addiction depends on it and depends only on Behavior Change plus Level-1 topics.
4. **Meaning (2.6).** Fourth because Level-3 Ritual and Long-term Adherence depend on it.
5. **Suffering (2.7).** Fifth because Recovery depends on it.
6. **Addiction (2.4).** Sixth because Recovery depends on it and it depends on Habits.
7. **Performance (2.5).** Seventh because it depends on Behavior Change plus Level-1 topics.
8. **Flourishing (2.8).** Eighth and last within Phase B because it depends on every other Level-2 topic.

### Phase C — Product Understanding (Level 3)

Order of execution:

1. **Commitments (3.2).** First because Witness depends on it, and it is closest to the company's core mechanism.
2. **Witness (3.3).** Second because it depends on Commitments and Self-Trust.
3. **Reflection (3.1).** Third because it depends on Self-Trust, Emotion, and Attention.
4. **Memory (3.4).** Fourth because it depends on Identity, Emotion, and Self-Trust.
5. **Feedback (3.6).** Fifth because it depends on Self-Trust, Emotion, and Behavior Change.
6. **Ritual (3.5).** Sixth because it depends on Meaning, Identity, and Habits.
7. **Intervention (3.9).** Seventh because it depends on Attention, Behavior Change, and Habits.
8. **Accountability (3.8).** Eighth because it depends on Self-Trust, Identity, and Motivation.
9. **Recovery (3.7).** Ninth because it depends on Suffering, Self-Trust, Addiction, and Emotion.
10. **Long-term Adherence (3.10).** Tenth and last because it depends on every Level-2 topic and integrates every prior Level-3 topic.

### Phase D — Atlas v1 Ratification

No new topics. See §6 review gates for the ratification procedure.

## §4 Milestones

Each phase closes on a named milestone.

- **M1 — Foundation Complete.** Every Level-1 topic (Human Nature, Attention, Emotion, Motivation, Decision Making, Identity) has at least one admitted Atlas entry per its expected outputs in RESEARCH_QUEUE. Dependency chain audit shows every Level-1 topic's stated dependencies satisfied.
- **M2 — Transformation Complete.** Every Level-2 topic (Self-Trust, Behavior Change, Habits, Meaning, Suffering, Addiction, Performance, Flourishing) has at least one admitted Atlas entry per its expected outputs. Dependency chain audit confirms every Level-2 topic's dependencies (Level-1 and intra-Level-2) are satisfied.
- **M3 — Product Understanding Complete.** Every Level-3 topic has at least one admitted Atlas entry per its expected outputs. Dependency chain audit confirms every Level-3 topic's dependencies (Level-1 and Level-2 and intra-Level-3) are satisfied.
- **M4 — Atlas v1 Complete.** Every RESEARCH_QUEUE topic has admitted entries. Every dependency across all three levels is honored. Every surfacing recorded in Stage 10 of the RESEARCH_EXECUTION workflow has been delivered to the Company Reference custodian. Atlas Version 1 is ratified.

## §5 Deliverables per Milestone

Each milestone produces a fixed deliverable set.

**M1 Deliverables**
- Six admitted Atlas entries (one per Level-1 topic, minimum).
- Six workflow logs (one per topic execution) archived per RESEARCH_EXECUTION.
- One Foundation Dependency Audit confirming Level-1 topics have their stated dependencies satisfied.
- Any surfacing records opened during Phase A, delivered to the Company Reference custodian.

**M2 Deliverables**
- Eight admitted Atlas entries (one per Level-2 topic, minimum).
- Eight workflow logs archived.
- One Transformation Dependency Audit confirming Level-2 topics' Level-1 and intra-Level-2 dependencies are satisfied.
- Any surfacing records opened during Phase B, delivered to the Company Reference custodian.

**M3 Deliverables**
- Ten admitted Atlas entries (one per Level-3 topic, minimum).
- Ten workflow logs archived.
- One Product Understanding Dependency Audit confirming Level-3 topics' Level-1, Level-2, and intra-Level-3 dependencies are satisfied.
- Any surfacing records opened during Phase C, delivered to the Company Reference custodian.

**M4 Deliverables**
- Atlas v1 Ratification Report — a single document listing every admitted entry, every dependency confirmed, every surfacing delivered, and every Open Question opened during the plan.
- Confirmation from the Founder Review, the Atlas Review, and the Company Reference Amendment Check gates (§6).

## §6 Review Gates

Every phase closes through three review gates. The phase does not close until all three gates pass.

**Gate G1 — Founder Review.**
- Purpose: confirm the phase's work aligns with the company's Purpose, Principles, and Strategy, and that no research has been suppressed, softened, or expanded beyond the sequence RESEARCH_QUEUE defines.
- Inputs: the phase's admitted Atlas entries and workflow logs.
- Output: a dated Founder Review record — pass, pass with noted concerns, or return to a specific workflow stage of a specific topic.

**Gate G2 — Atlas Review.**
- Purpose: confirm the phase's admitted entries meet the standards defined in RESEARCH_METHOD and follow the format defined in RESEARCH_METHOD §7. Confirm no contradiction was silently reconciled, no minority evidence dropped, no Unknown or Insufficient outcome hidden.
- Inputs: the phase's admitted entries, peer-review records from Stage 9, and the Dependency Audit for the phase.
- Output: a dated Atlas Review record — pass, pass with noted corrections, or return specific entries to the Stage 9 peer-review gate.

**Gate G3 — Company Reference Amendment Check.**
- Purpose: confirm that every surfacing recorded by Stage 10 during the phase has been delivered to the Company Reference custodian, and that no amendment to the Company Reference has been made outside the formal amendment process.
- Inputs: the phase's Stage 10 surfacing records; the Company Reference Decision Log for the phase's window.
- Output: a dated Amendment Check record — pass, or return with a specific surfacing that requires delivery.

A phase closes when G1, G2, and G3 all pass. If any gate returns work, the returned work is completed and re-submitted; the gate is re-run until pass.

## §7 Completion Criteria

The plan is complete when all of the following hold:

- Every topic in RESEARCH_QUEUE Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 has at least one admitted Atlas entry per its expected outputs.
- Every dependency stated in RESEARCH_QUEUE is honored: for each topic, every prerequisite topic has an admitted entry at a confidence adequate to interpret the dependent investigation, or the dependent entry carries a documented note that the prerequisite is not yet satisfied.
- Every Stage 10 surfacing record generated during the plan has been delivered to the Company Reference custodian, and the delivery has been logged.
- Every phase (Phase A, Phase B, Phase C, Phase D) has passed all three review gates (G1, G2, G3).
- The Atlas v1 Ratification Report has been produced and archived.

Completion does not close Atlas. Atlas remains open and append-only past the plan's completion; every RESEARCH_QUEUE topic remains a permanent research direction per the RESEARCH_QUEUE Governing Rules. RESEARCH_PLAN §1's objective — Atlas Version 1 — is a first-population milestone, not a terminal state of understanding.
